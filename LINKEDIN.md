## 🇬🇧 English

**What if you could build your own Palantir Gotham with open-source tools?**

That's exactly what I did.

I built **AEGIS** — a critical infrastructure intelligence platform that replicates the architectural patterns behind **Palantir Gotham** and **Microsoft Fabric IQ**: knowledge graphs, OWL ontologies, heterogeneous data fusion, AI agents, and hidden link discovery.

The key is an **OWL 2.0 ontology** that models the same patterns an intelligence analyst uses — persons, organizations, facilities, events, substances, sensors and their relationships — so AI agents reason over the graph the way a human would, but in seconds.

The scenario: **water supply security** — 20 facilities, 100 persons, 6 sabotage scenarios, from chemical manipulation to SCADA cyberattacks.

🔍 4 specialized AI agents (military, civil, operations, automated alerts)
🧠 Natural language → real-time graph queries
🕸️ Hidden link discovery between persons, facilities, and events
📡 8 threat detection patterns running every 30 seconds
🗺️ 10 intelligence document types fused into a single search surface

Fully local. No cloud. One `docker compose up`.

> *Ever wanted to be a security analyst for a day? Investigate a coordinated sabotage across two water treatment plants, discover that a contractor and an employee are linked through a shell organization, all from a single natural language question?*

The repo is public, the code is documented as a research article, and the platform spins up in minutes.

**Neo4j · OpenSearch · .NET 10 · React 19 · Ollama · OWL 2.0 · Docker**
